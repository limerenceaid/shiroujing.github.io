Shirou Jing

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at Rice University, advised by Prof. Tony (Tong) Geng. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS).

Before Rice, I worked on generative / diffusion models (controllable image & video generation) at UCAS with Dr. Zhenliang He and Prof. Shiguang Shan, and interned at UC Berkeley's ABC Lab (Betzig lab) on AI for microscopy imaging.

My research focuses on information-efficient, maturity-aware pretraining of language models — designing dynamic learning tasks so that models begin to acquire reasoning capability from the pretraining stage — and on multi-agent LLM systems.

Selected Publications

InfoDLM: an Information-Adaptive Framework for Discrete Diffusion Language Model Pretraining
Shirou Jing, Chunshu Wu, Chuan Liu, Arghavan Bahadorinejad, Feitong Qiao, Dongfang Liu, Tony Geng
ICML 2026
OpenReview / PDF

InfoDLM replaces the heuristic random masking in diffusion-LM pretraining with an information-gain–driven mask policy (a trainable IG signal + maturity-aware masking, jointly trained with the model). With ~7.6% FLOPs overhead, it improves reasoning accuracy by up to 13% over LLaDA-472M at a comparable budget.

Learning with Noisy Labels via Mamba and Entropy KNN Framework
Ningwei Wang, Weiqiang Jin, Shirou Jing, Haixia Bi, Guang Yang
Applied Soft Computing, 2025  /  paper

RelabelMamba: a learning-with-noisy-labels framework with a bidirectional state-space (Mamba) backbone for global, linear-complexity representations; entropy–variance + KNN relabeling (AEKR) to correct mislabeled samples; and a variance-aware mixup (Adaptpuzzle). Competitive on CIFAR-10/100, Clothing1M, and ANIMAL-10N.

Diffusion-Based Generative Self-Supervised Model for Few-Shot PolSAR Image Classification
Kang Liu, Zuzheng Kuang, Shirou Jing, Haixia Bi, Yaochen Li, Chen Xu
IGARSS 2024  /  IEEE Xplore

A diffusion-based generative self-supervised model that learns discriminative, robust polarimetric representations without annotations via a noise-adding / denoising process, improving few-shot PolSAR image classification.

GIGT: Gesture-Recognition-Integrated Game Therapy for Tenosynovitis
Shirou Jing, Xuejin Wu, Wei Sun
IPCV 2024
project page / arXiv

An interactive game for tenosynovitis prevention: deep-learning hand-gesture recognition drives customizable tendon-sheath exercises through an engaging interface.

Preprints & In Submission

RCS: Rhythm-Controlled Communication for Multi-Agent LLM Systems
Submitted to NeurIPS 2026

Reformulates communication in multi-agent LLM systems from fixed, top-down turn-taking into a learned, self-adaptive communication rhythm — coordinating when agents contribute, not just what — reaching debate-level accuracy at a fraction of the communication cost.

CtrLoRA-XL: Cost-efficient, High-fidelity Multi-condition Controllable Image Generation on Stable Diffusion XL
With Dr. Zhenliang He and Prof. Shiguang Shan
Submitted to IJCV

A cost-efficient, high-fidelity framework for multi-condition controllable image generation on SDXL, cutting GPU and training-image requirements by over 99% for adapting to novel conditions while preserving fidelity.

Research Journey

My research journey has been driven by leveraging computer science and AI to build solutions that intersect with human needs — from early inspirations in science literature to hands-on work in startups and research institutions, exploring generative models, human–computer interaction, and practical applications of AI.

My fascination with science began in childhood (books like Horrible Science and Horrible Maths), grew during a six-month visit to OSU where I built a toy rocket, and sharpened in high school through informatics competitions (provincial first prize). At university I built a foundation in math and physics, which proved pivotal during a summer internship at ZhenTec Intelligence, a startup incubated by Xi'an Jiaotong University, where I worked on brain–machine rehabilitation robots.

My interests later expanded to lip-reading recognition, video-forgery detection, and radar image processing. At Xi'an Jiaotong University, with Prof. Haixia Bi, I worked on classifying Polarimetric SAR images using diffusion models (published at IGARSS 2024). Motivated by tenosynovitis among lab mates, I collaborated with Prof. Feng Tian and Prof. Wei Sun on a game-based rehabilitation solution (a patent application and the GIGT paper at IPCV). My interest in generative models deepened during collaboration with Prof. Shiguang Shan and Dr. Zhenliang He at the Institute of Computing Technology, supported by RMB 67,000 from China's first open grant project for undergraduates.

An exchange visit to UC Berkeley (interning at the ABC lab, founded by Eric Betzig) further refined my trajectory, working on AI-driven microscopy image analysis and reinforcing my commitment to advanced study in AI.
